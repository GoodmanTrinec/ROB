# Hardware draft — v0.1

## Core
- Raspberry Pi 5 8 GB
- ESP32 DevKit

## Drive
- 2× 12 V DC geared motor with encoder
- 2WD or 4WD metal chassis
- Cytron MDD10A or equivalent dual motor driver

## Sensors / I/O
- USB 1080p camera
- USB microphone
- small speaker

## Power
- 12 V battery pack with BMS
- main fuse
- emergency stop switch
- 12 V → 5 V DC/DC converter, >= 5 A
- XT60 connectors

## Important
Final battery chemistry, motor current, fuse rating, wire gauge and DC/DC sizing must be selected only after the exact motors and chassis are chosen.
